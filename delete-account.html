Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Realr account and all associated data at any time. Here's how.

How to delete from the app

1

Open the Realr app and go to the Profile tab

2

Scroll down to the Account section

3

Tap Delete Account

4

Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete Forever

What gets deleted

All of the following data is permanently and immediately removed:

Account & profile Posts Messages Connections Block list Subscription info Device tokens Flag history Activity log

⚠ Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. There is no recovery period.

Can't access the app? Email support@realrapp.com from the email address linked to your account and we'll process your deletion request.
